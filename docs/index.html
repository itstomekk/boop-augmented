Boop Augmented 🎄

Rules 🎄

The tree is seen from above: the centre is the top, each ring outward is one tier lower. The ⭐ marks the treetop, the single cell of tier 1. Kittens and cats can sit there; ornaments can never get up there.

  1. Golden rule: if you have no ornament on the tree, start your turn by hanging one on tier 2 (gold dashed ring). Ornaments do not boop.
  2. Then place a kitten (or a cat) on any free cell.
  3. Boop! The new piece pushes every neighbour (8 directions) one cell away, if the cell behind it is free. A kitten or cat pushed off the tree goes back to its owner.
  4. Kittens cannot boop cats. Cats boop everything.
  5. Ornaments move sideways or down the tree, never up. Knock off your opponent's ornament and you capture it; knock off your own and you get it back.
  6. Pounce: a cat pushed onto an ornament takes it (captured if it is the opponent's, returned if it is its own) and goes back to its owner's pool. A kitten pushed onto an ornament stays put.
  7. Three of your pieces in a row leave the tree and the kittens become cats. If several lines form, you pick one.
  8. All your pieces on the tree? Take one back; it returns as a cat.

Win nice: three cats in a row, or all your pieces on the tree as cats. Win naughty: capture 3 of your opponent's ornaments.

Big tree: same rules, 5 to 8 tiers. Kittens on Auto: 8 on the classic tree, 2 more per extra tier (16 on 8 tiers). Ornaments on Auto: more of them (up to 6), up to 3 hanging at once, hung on the 3rd tier from the bottom so they stay in reach. Capturing 3 still wins. 3D view: drag the board to orbit (left/right turns, up/down tilts), scroll to zoom, or use the ⟲ ⟳ ⤒ ⤓ − + ⌂ buttons and the Q/E, W/S, +/- keys. Keys: K kitten, C cat, U undo.
